# Tile face artwork — credits

The mahjong tile-face images in this directory — `Man1`–`Man9`, `Pin1`–`Pin9`,
`Sou1`–`Sou9`, and the honors `Ton`/`Nan`/`Shaa`/`Pei`/`Chun`/`Hatsu`/`Haku` —
are cropped from a photograph of a real tile set:

- **Title:** Mahjong eg Hokkian.jpg
- **Author:** Cangjie6
- **Source:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mahjong_eg_Hokkian.jpg
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (CC BY-SA 4.0) — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

**Changes made:** the source photo (`Mahjong_eg_Hokkian.jpg`, kept in this folder
for reference) was split into the individual tiles, each cropped to its face,
given rounded transparent corners, and exported as a PNG.

Because the source is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, these derived tile images are
distributed under the **same CC BY-SA 4.0 license**.
