Game Boy Advance

Add .gba or .zip ROM files once — they are stored in the browser and playable offline. In game: RT = quick save, LT = quick load, hold R3 = fast forward (rebind in the settings gear → Control Settings). Core defaults to mGBA; switch a ROM to VBA-M if its in-game saving fails under mGBA (e.g. some Chinese bootleg hacks).
