Nintendo DS

Add .nds or .zip ROM files once — they are stored in the browser and playable offline. In game: RT = quick save, LT = quick load, hold R3 = fast forward; tap the lower screen directly for touch input. Screen layout and other options are in the settings gear menu. Tap ⋯ on a ROM for cloud backup/restore, or to clear its quick-saves (if a game freezes on quick-load) or delete it.
