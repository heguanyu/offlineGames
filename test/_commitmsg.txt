国标: fix 听 detection, auto-play once 听, enlarge the 打出/碰 prompt

- 听 detection (handWaits) now uses the self-draw context (which includes
  自摸 / 不求人 / 暗刻), so a hand that's ready by self-draw counts as 听 even
  when winning off a discard would fall short of the fan minimum. (minFan was
  already wired through; that part was fine.)
- The 听牌 hint is now proactive: while choosing a discard it shows which tiles
  put you 听 ("可听！打 X 即听"), not just for the selected tile.
- New rule: once the human reaches 听, the seat is locked and auto-plays — each
  draw is auto-discarded (tsumogiri) and wins are taken automatically, until the
  hand ends. No manual discard/claim while 听.
- The 打出 button (and its hint) are now doubled in size and pinned at the same
  front-center spot as the 碰 prompt, in 天津 and 国标.
